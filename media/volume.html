Sky volume · 23 Nov 2026, 10:39–10:44 UTC

A 3D model of the sky above the Jetson and Pi stations during one five-minute Jetson clip. It is built from the two cameras, ADS-B, Meteosat and a weather model's wind. The cirrus is a lit volume: its base comes from Cloudnet, its top from Meteosat, and its structure from the Jetson's real frames. It also shows every aircraft within 45 km and the contrails. Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and press play to run the clip.
